# Known Issues

> Self-maintained. This is the FIRST place to look on any unexpected failure
> (AGENTS.md R5) and the place to record a fix the moment you find one (R3).
> Append the next numbered entry; never renumber or delete resolved entries unless
> pruning per AGENTS.md section 5. Entries include failed approaches so no future
> session repeats them.
>
> Two kinds of entry live here:
> - **Project issues** - errors specific to this codebase and their fixes.
> - **Agent operational lessons** - generic "don't do X, do Y" tool/workflow lessons
>   discovered while working (keep these project-agnostic).
